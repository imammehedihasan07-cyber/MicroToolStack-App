❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are MicroToolStack tools completely free to use?

Yes, every tool on MicroToolStack is 100% free to use. There are no registration requirements, premium paywalls, or hidden charges.

Do I need to create an account to access features?

No, you do not need an account. You can use any utility instantly as soon as you visit the site.

Are my uploaded files and text data secure?

Yes. The majority of MicroToolStack tools operate entirely on client-side JavaScript. This means your text and files are processed locally within your web browser and are never transmitted to external servers.

Which devices and operating systems are supported?

MicroToolStack is fully responsive and compatible with all modern web browsers on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.

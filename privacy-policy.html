Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Processing

At MicroToolStack, we prioritize user privacy. All client-side text tools, JSON formatters, and character counters process data entirely inside your web browser. Your input text is never uploaded, saved, or stored on our servers.

2. Analytics & Cookies

We may use minimal privacy-compliant cookies or anonymous analytics to monitor performance and visitor traffic to improve page speed and functionality.

3. AI Utilities

When using our AI tools, your text query is submitted to our backend function via serverless API solely to return AI generation responses. Prompts are not stored or sold to third parties.

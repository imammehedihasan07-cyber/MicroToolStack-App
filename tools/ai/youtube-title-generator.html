Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a good Click-Through Rate (CTR) on YouTube?

An average CTR on YouTube ranges between 2% and 10%. Highly viral videos with exceptional title and thumbnail synergy often see CTRs well above 10% within the first 24 hours of upload.

Should I put numbers and emojis in my YouTube title?

Yes! Adding numbers (e.g., "Top 5 Tips" or "In 24 Hours") and strategic emojis can increase visual attention and boost clicks significantly on mobile screens.

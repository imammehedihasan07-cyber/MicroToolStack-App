Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long should a YouTube video description be?

YouTube allows up to 5,000 characters in a video description. Aiming for 150 to 300 words with primary keywords in the first two lines works best for search rankings.

Are video tags still useful on YouTube?

While titles and thumbnails drive CTR, tags help YouTube categorize videos, especially if your target keyword contains common spelling variations or colloquial terms.

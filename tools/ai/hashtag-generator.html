Why Hashtags Are Essential for Social Media Reach

Hashtags act as a digital tagging system for social media algorithms. When you post a reel on Instagram or a short video on TikTok, hashtags tell search engines and discovery recommendation algorithms exactly what your content is about. Using targeted, low-to-medium competition hashtags alongside high-volume viral tags increases your chances of landing on the Explore page or TikTok's For You Page (FYP).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?

While Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post, social media experts recommend using 3 to 8 highly relevant hashtags for optimal reach and clean post aesthetics.

Should I put hashtags in the caption or in the comments?

Both methods work effectively! Placing hashtags in either the caption or the first comment allows social media algorithms to index your post content properly.

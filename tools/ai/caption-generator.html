Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long should an Instagram caption be?

Short captions (1 to 3 lines) work best for funny or aesthetic reels, while longer storytelling captions (100+ words) work well for educational posts, lifestyle updates, and high-engagement carousels.

Do hashtags in captions still help reach new viewers?

Yes! Combining 3 to 7 niche-specific hashtags in your caption helps social algorithms classify your content and display it on search explore pages.

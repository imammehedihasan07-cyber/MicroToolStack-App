Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long should an Instagram bio be?

Instagram limits bios to 150 characters. It is best to use bullet points or short broken lines with emojis to make the most of the limited space.

Can I use special fonts in my generated bios?

Yes! You can copy the generated text and apply unique custom typography using Unicode font tools available in our utility library.

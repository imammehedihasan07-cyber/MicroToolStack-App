Understanding Payment Gateway Merchant Processing Fees

When running an e-commerce store, freelancing business, digital agency, or SaaS platform, processing credit card payments via PayPal or Stripe involves transaction processing fees. Every time a client pays an invoice, payment gateways deduct a combination of a percentage rate (e.g., 2.9%) and a small fixed transaction fee (e.g., $0.30).

Our free PayPal & Stripe Fee Calculator helps merchants and freelancers understand exact profit margins. It calculates the net payout you will receive after fees, as well as the exact gross amount you must charge your client to cover transaction fees completely.

Standard Payment Gateway Fee Formulas Explained

Calculating payment gateway deductions requires applying two primary financial formulas:

1. Net Take-Home Amount Formula

When you send a client an invoice for a set dollar amount ($A$), the payment processor calculates the fee and net payout using:

$$\text{Processing Fee} = (A \times \text{Percentage Rate}) + \text{Fixed Fee}$$

$$\text{Net Received} = A - \text{Processing Fee}$$

2. Required Invoice (Gross-Up) Formula

If you want to receive an exact net amount ($N$) in your bank account after fees are subtracted, you must invoice a slightly higher gross amount ($G$):

$$G = \frac{N + \text{Fixed Fee}}{1 - \text{Percentage Rate}}$$

Example: To take home exactly $100.00 on Stripe domestic (2.9% + $0.30), you must invoice the client **$103.30**.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Stripe's standard transaction fee?

Stripe's standard domestic credit card rate is 2.9% + $0.30 per successful transaction. International cards incur an extra +1.5% cross-border fee.

What is PayPal's standard merchant commercial fee?

PayPal's standard US domestic commercial transaction fee is 2.99% + $0.49 per transaction, with cross-border international sales incurring additional percentage fees.

Is my transaction or payment data stored on your server?

No. All calculations run client-side inside your web browser using JavaScript. No financial inputs or invoice values are logged or uploaded.

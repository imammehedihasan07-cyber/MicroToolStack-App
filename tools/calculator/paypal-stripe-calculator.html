Understanding Merchant Payment Processing Fees

When accepting payments online through Stripe or PayPal, transaction fees can quickly add up. Knowing the exact percentage and fixed fees per transaction allows freelancers, e-commerce sellers, and agencies to set accurate pricing and avoid profit loss.

Standard Gateway Fee Structures

  • Stripe Domestic: Charges 2.9% + $0.30 per successful credit card payment within the same country.
  • Stripe International: Adds an additional 1% cross-border fee for international card processing (3.9% + $0.30).
  • PayPal Commercial: Standard US merchant transactions usually start at 2.99% + $0.49 fixed fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the "Invoice Amount" calculated?

The invoice calculation uses reverse fee mathematics: (Target Amount + Fixed Fee) / (1 - Fee Percentage) to ensure that after deductions, your net payout matches your target rate.

How to Calculate Discounts & Sale Prices

Whether shopping online, during Black Friday sales, or at retail stores, knowing how much you actually save with percentage discounts helps you make smarter purchasing decisions. Discounts are usually expressed as a percentage off the original price.

Discount Calculation Formula

  • Saved Amount: Savings = Original Price × (Discount / 100)
  • Discounted Price: Sale Price = Original Price - Savings
  • With Sales Tax: Final Price = Sale Price + (Sale Price × (Tax / 100))

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 20% discount on $100?

Multiply 100 by 0.20 to get $20 savings. Subtracting $20 from $100 leaves a final sale price of $80.

Is tax calculated before or after the discount?

In almost all retail transactions, sales tax is applied to the final discounted price rather than the original item price.

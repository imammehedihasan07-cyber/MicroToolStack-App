What is Body Mass Index (BMI)?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a standardized measurement used to estimate whether a person has a healthy body weight for their height. By dividing your weight by the square of your height, our BMI Calculator instantly evaluates your score against international health categories (Underweight, Normal Weight, Overweight, and Obese).

BMI Classification Categories

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9: Normal Weight (Healthy Range)
  • 25.0 – 29.9: Overweight
  • 30.0 and Above: Obese

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI score range?

A BMI score between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered a normal, healthy weight range for adults according to World Health Organization (WHO) standards.

Is my health data private?

Yes, all calculations run locally in your browser using JavaScript. No personal weight or height inputs are ever sent to external servers.

How CGPA to Percentage Conversion Works

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is an academic evaluation metric used globally by schools, colleges, and higher education institutes. Since different institutions follow distinct grading standard scales (such as 4.0, 5.0, or 10.0), converting CGPA into a percentage helps standardize evaluation during job applications, scholarship requests, and university admissions.

Conversion Formulas Explained

  • 10.0 Grading Scale (Standard CBSE Method): Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
  • 4.0 Grading Scale (US Standard): Percentage = (CGPA / 4.0) × 100
  • 5.0 Grading Scale: Percentage = (CGPA / 5.0) × 100
  • Direct Percentage Method: Percentage = CGPA × 10 (used by select technical institutes)

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is CGPA multiplied by 9.5 on a 10-point scale?

The multiplier 9.5 was derived by educational boards (such as CBSE) through statistical analysis comparing overall performance data to align grade ranges accurately with percentages.

Can I calculate total marks from CGPA?

Yes! Simply enter your total maximum marks in the calculator above, and it will compute your estimated obtained marks based on your CGPA percentage.

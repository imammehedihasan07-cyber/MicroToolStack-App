Understanding CGPA and Percentage Conversions

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is an academic grading metric used by universities, secondary education boards (like CBSE), and international institutions to summarize a student's overall academic performance. However, job applications, higher education entry forms, and scholarship portals frequently mandate marks submitted as a percentage (%).

Because different academic institutions implement varying conversion factors, our free CGPA to Percentage Calculator supports standard 10-point scales ($CGPA \times 9.5$), AICTE engineering formulas ($(CGPA - 0.75) \times 10$), and 4.0 GPA standards to ensure accurate mark conversion.

Standard Formulas for CGPA to Percentage Calculation

Depending on your board or university guidelines, use the appropriate conversion formula:

1. Standard CBSE 10.0 CGPA Scale

The standard formula established by secondary education boards multiplies the 10-point CGPA score by 9.5:

$$\text{Percentage (\%)} = \text{CGPA} \times 9.5$$

Example: A CGPA of 8.2 equals $8.2 \times 9.5 = 77.9\%$.

2. AICTE Technical / Engineering Formula

The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) applies a subtracted benchmark formula for technical diplomas and engineering degrees:

$$\text{Percentage (\%)} = (\text{CGPA} - 0.75) \times 10$$

Example: A CGPA of 8.0 equals $(8.0 - 0.75) \times 10 = 72.5\%$.

3. 4.0 US / International GPA Scale

For international universities utilizing a 4-point GPA system, percentage is calculated using direct ratio scaling:

$$\text{Percentage (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{GPA}}{4.0}\right) \times 100$$

Why Rotate PDF Pages?

Scanned documents or photographs often end up sideways or upside down. Our free PDF Rotate tool allows you to instantly fix document orientations so your files read correctly on all devices.

How to Rotate PDF Files Online

Follow these quick steps:

  • Step 1: Upload your PDF document into the dropzone.
  • Step 2: Choose your desired rotation angle (90°, 180°, or 270°).
  • Step 3: Click "Rotate PDF & Download" to save your corrected document.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your documents remain completely secure. All rotation processing runs locally inside your web browser session using JavaScript without uploading files to remote servers.

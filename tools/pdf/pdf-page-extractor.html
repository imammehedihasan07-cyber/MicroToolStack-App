Why Use an Online PDF Page Extractor?

Often you only need specific pages from a large PDF report, textbook, or document. Instead of sending or storing the entire heavy file, our free PDF Page Extractor allows you to pull out precisely the pages you need into a brand-new PDF file instantly.

How to Extract Pages from PDF Documents

Follow these simple steps:

  • Step 1: Upload your PDF document into the dropzone.
  • Step 2: Type the specific page numbers or ranges you want to extract (e.g., 1, 3, 5-8).
  • Step 3: Click "Extract Pages & Download" to get your new concise document.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your documents remain completely secure. All PDF extraction operations run locally inside your web browser session using JavaScript without uploading files to remote servers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I specify page ranges?

You can enter single pages separated by commas (e.g., 1, 4, 9) or use hyphens for consecutive ranges (e.g., 2-6).

Are my files safe and private?

Yes. All processing happens entirely within your browser session. Your documents are never uploaded anywhere.

Is this tool free?

Yes, MicroToolStack's PDF Page Extractor is 100% free with no limits or registration required.

Why Add Page Numbers to PDF Files?

When preparing academic theses, reports, legal documents, or eBooks, clean pagination is essential for readability and formal presentation. Our free PDF Page Numbering Tool stamps sequential page numbers onto your documents instantly.

How to Number PDF Pages Online

Follow these quick steps:

  • Step 1: Upload your PDF document into the upload dropzone.
  • Step 2: Select your preferred position (e.g., bottom-center) and display format.
  • Step 3: Click "Add Page Numbers & Download" to save your updated file.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your documents remain completely secure. All pagination editing runs locally inside your web browser session without uploading files to remote servers.

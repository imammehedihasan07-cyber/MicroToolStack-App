Why Rearrange PDF Pages?

Sometimes scanned documents, reports, or chapters get misordered during scanning or compilation. Our free PDF Reorder Pages tool allows you to easily shuffle and sort page sequences into the exact order you need.

How to Reorder PDF Pages Online

Follow these simple steps:

  • Step 1: Upload your PDF document into the dropzone.
  • Step 2: Type your desired new page sequence separated by commas (e.g., 2, 1, 3-5).
  • Step 3: Click "Reorder Pages & Download" to get your restructured document.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your documents remain completely secure. All page sorting runs locally inside your web browser session using JavaScript without uploading files to remote servers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I specify the new page order?

Type the page numbers in your preferred order separated by commas. You can also include ranges (e.g., 5, 1-4, 6).

Are my files safe and private?

Yes. All processing happens entirely within your browser session using client-side JavaScript.

Is this tool free?

Yes, MicroToolStack's PDF Reorder Pages tool is 100% free with no limits or registration required.

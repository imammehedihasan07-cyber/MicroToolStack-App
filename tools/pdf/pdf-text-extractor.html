Why Use an Online PDF Text Extractor?

Sometimes documents are locked or difficult to copy text from directly. Our free PDF Text Extractor scans your PDF files and pulls out all readable text strings, allowing you to copy or download them instantly as a plain text file.

How to Extract Text from PDF Files

Follow these quick steps:

  • Step 1: Upload your PDF document into the dropzone.
  • Step 2: Wait a moment for the tool to parse and display the extracted text.
  • Step 3: Click "Copy Text" or "Download TXT" to use your content.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your documents remain completely secure. All text extraction runs locally inside your web browser session using JavaScript without uploading files to remote servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I enter custom page numbers?
A: You can type single pages separated by commas (e.g., "1, 4, 7") or continuous page ranges with hyphens (e.g., "2-6").

Q: Is my document kept secure?
A: Yes, completely. The extraction happens 100% inside your device browser, so no sensitive documents leave your device.

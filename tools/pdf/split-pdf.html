Why Splitting PDF Documents and Extracting Pages is Necessary

Large Portable Document Format (PDF) files—such as e-books, multi-chapter research publications, scanned contracts, or lengthy academic textbooks—often contain pages you do not need to share or print. Extracting specific sections or splitting a large PDF into targeted documents saves bandwidth, reduces storage space, and preserves privacy.

Instead of emailing a 100-page document when only a 3-page summary is required, splitting the document lets you send concise, focused files. Our free Online PDF Page Splitter extracts your requested page ranges directly inside your web browser.

Custom Page Range Syntax Options

Flexible extraction formatting allows you to select precise page combinations:

  • Single Pages: Enter comma-separated page numbers (e.g., 1, 4, 7) to extract distinct pages.
  • Sequential Ranges: Use hyphens to define start and end pages (e.g., 1-5) to extract consecutive sections.
  • Combined Extractions: Combine ranges and individual pages (e.g., 1-3, 5, 8-10) into a single extracted output file.

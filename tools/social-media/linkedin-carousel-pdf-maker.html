Why LinkedIn PDF Carousels Drive Maximum Engagement

PDF carousels are one of the highest-performing content formats on LinkedIn. By structuring information into digestible, swipeable slides, creators encourage users to spend more time on their posts—significantly boosting **dwell time** and algorithmic reach.

Best Practices for Creating High-Converting Carousels

  • Hooking First Slide: Use bold, clear titles on Slide 1 to grab feed attention.
  • Concise Text: Keep text brief—use 1 main takeaway per slide.
  • Consistent Branding: Maintain matching background colors and your social handle across all slides.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload the exported PDF to LinkedIn?

When creating a new LinkedIn post, click the "Document" (PDF) icon, select your downloaded PDF file, and write your post caption.

What is the recommended number of slides?

Carousels with 4 to 8 slides perform best for engagement and scroll depth.

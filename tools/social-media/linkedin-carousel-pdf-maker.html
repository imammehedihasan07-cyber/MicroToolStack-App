Best Practices for High-Converting LinkedIn Carousels

Follow these expert tips when structuring your carousel presentation deck:

  • Hook Them on Slide 1: Your first slide title should present a compelling hook, question, or promise to capture user attention instantly.
  • Keep Text Concise: Use short bullet points and bold headings. Avoid dense paragraphs that are difficult to read on mobile devices.
  • Include a Call-to-Action (CTA): End your final slide with a clear CTA encouraging users to comment, connect, or follow your profile.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I upload a carousel PDF to LinkedIn?

Create a new post on LinkedIn, click the '+' or 'More' icon, select 'Add document', upload your downloaded PDF file, add a caption, and publish!

What is the best aspect ratio for LinkedIn carousels?

Square (1:1 ratio, such as 1080 x 1080 pixels) or portrait (4:5 ratio) documents perform exceptionally well on both desktop and mobile LinkedIn feeds.

Is my slide data saved on remote servers?

No. All PDF generation runs client-side inside your browser session using JavaScript. Your text remains 100% private.

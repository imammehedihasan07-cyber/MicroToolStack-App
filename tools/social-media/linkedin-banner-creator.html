The ideal size for a personal LinkedIn profile banner is 1584 pixels wide by 396 pixels tall.

How do I upload my downloaded banner to LinkedIn?

Go to your LinkedIn profile, click the pencil edit icon in the top right corner of your header background, and upload your downloaded PNG image.

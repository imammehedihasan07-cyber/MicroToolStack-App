Why Clean URL Slugs Matter for Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

A URL slug is the precise part of a web address that follows your domain name (e.g., in https://example.com/tools/seo/slug-generator, the slug is slug-generator). Crafting concise, readable slugs containing target keywords helps search engines like Google understand page context while providing human users with clear expectations of content before they click.

Messy URLs filled with uppercase letters, special symbols, spaces, or random tracking parameters create poor user experiences and can lead to duplicate content indexing confusion. Our free Online URL Slug Generator sanitizes text into clean, search-friendly slugs instantly.

Best Practices for Creating SEO-Friendly URL Slugs

Follow these essential guidelines when structuring webpage slugs:

  • Keep It Short & Descriptive: Limit slugs to 3 to 5 core words that accurately summarize the webpage topic.
  • Use Hyphens (-) to Separate Words: Search engines recognize hyphens as word separators. Avoid underscores or spaces in URLs.
  • Always Use Lowercase Letters: Mixed-case URLs can sometimes cause duplicate content issues on case-sensitive web servers.
  • Remove Stop Words: Strip out unnecessary minor words (such as "and", "the", "of", "to") to keep URLs clean and punchy.

How do I convert a title into a URL slug?

Should I use hyphens or underscores in URL slugs?

Google officially recommends using hyphens (-) to separate words in URLs. Underscores (_) can sometimes prevent search engines from parsing individual words correctly.

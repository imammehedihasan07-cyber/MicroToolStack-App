What Makes a Great SEO URL Slug?

A URL Slug is the user-facing portion of a website link that describes a page's content. Creating clean, hyphenated slugs without uppercase letters, accents, or special symbols makes your links easier for humans to read and for Google bops to index efficiently.

URL Slug Best Practices

  • Use Hyphens as Separators: Always separate words with hyphens (-) rather than underscores (_).
  • Keep it Short & Focused: Remove unnecessary stop words (e.g., "and", "the", "for") to focus on target keywords.
  • Use Lowercase Letters: Avoid capital letters to prevent 404 redirection issues on case-sensitive web servers.
  • Strip Special Characters: Remove punctuation, symbols, and non-ASCII accents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I avoid special characters in URLs?

Special characters like ?, #, %, &, and @ have specific structural functions in web servers and browsers. Including them in slugs causes broken links and ugly encoded URL strings.

Can I change a URL slug after publishing a page?

Yes, but changing a slug alters the page URL. You must set up a 301 redirect from the old URL to the new slug to avoid losing existing SEO traffic and backlink authority.

Why Testing Open Graph Snippets Improves Social Media Click-Through Rates

Social media feeds are visual environments. When users share your links on platforms like Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), LinkedIn, or Discord, the platform inspects your HTML head tags for Open Graph (OG) metadata to generate a rich preview snippet.

If your Open Graph title is too long, or if your og:image thumbnail is improperly sized or missing, social networks will render broken, unappealing text-only cards. Using our free Online Open Graph Preview Tool allows webmasters to test and refine social share cards before publishing content live.

Recommended Social Share Card Specs

Follow these official dimensions across major social networks:

  • Facebook Link Cards: Requires an og:image ratio of 1.91:1, ideally 1200 x 630 pixels. Titles render clearly up to 60-70 characters.
  • Twitter / X Summary Large Image: Requires a 2:1 image ratio (at least 600 x 300 px, ideally 1200 x 600 px) with twitter:card set to summary_large_image.
  • LinkedIn Feed Share: Uses an og:image recommendation of 1200 x 627 pixels.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I preview how my link looks on social media?

Type or paste your Title, Page URL, Image URL, and Description into the input boxes. Select Facebook, Twitter, or LinkedIn tabs to inspect live share cards.

Why doesn't my social image thumbnail load in the preview?

Ensure your og:image URL is an absolute link starting with https:// and that the server hosting the image allows cross-origin loading.

Is my input data saved on remote servers?

No. All calculations run client-side inside your browser session using JavaScript. Your inputs remain 100% private.

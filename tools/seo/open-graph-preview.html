Why Open Graph Tags are Essential for Social Traffic

Open Graph (OG) protocol tags allow web developers to control how web content presents when shared on social networks like Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, and messaging apps. Without OG tags, social networks automatically guess title and image elements, frequently resulting in missing thumbnails or broken descriptions.

Core Open Graph Protocol Meta Tags

  • og:title — The headline title of your article or page.
  • og:description — A brief 2 to 3 sentence summary of your content.
  • og:image — The featured thumbnail image (optimal dimensions: 1200 x 630 pixels).
  • og:url — The canonical web URL for social engagement counters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my image not updating on Facebook?

Facebook caches Open Graph share data for several days. You can force Facebook to refresh cached metadata using the official Facebook Sharing Debugger tool.

Where should I place these meta tags?

Paste the generated Open Graph meta tags inside the <head>...</head> section of your HTML document.

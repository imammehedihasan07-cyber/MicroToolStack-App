Why Open Graph Meta Tags Matter for Traffic

When users share your web links on Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or Telegram, social platforms scan your page's HTML for Open Graph (og:) Tags. Proper OG tags ensure your shared links display eye-catching preview cards with full-width images, titles, and descriptions, significantly increasing referral click-through rates.

Essential Open Graph Tags

  • og:title: The main headline of your content as it should appear in social feeds.
  • og:description: A brief 1–2 sentence summary encouraging users to click your link.
  • og:image: High-resolution banner image URL (Recommended size: 1200 x 630 pixels).
  • twitter:card: Enables Twitter/X summary cards with large images (summary_large_image).

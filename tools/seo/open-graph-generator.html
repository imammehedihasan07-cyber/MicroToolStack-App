Why Open Graph Meta Tags Drive Social Media Engagement

Introduced by Facebook, the Open Graph Protocol (OG) enables any webpage to become a rich object in social media feeds. When users share your website links on Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, or WhatsApp, social platforms read your webpage's Open Graph meta tags to render eye-catching preview cards featuring your headline, description, domain name, and thumbnail image.

Without Open Graph tags, social platforms fall back to extracting arbitrary body text or unoptimized images, resulting in unappealing link posts. Our free Online Open Graph Generator crafts standardized Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags with live social card previews.

Core Open Graph Meta Tags Specification

Essential Open Graph properties to include in your HTML <head> section:

  • og:title: The main headline displayed on social newsfeed preview cards.
  • og:type: The type of media object (e.g. website, article, or product).
  • og:image: The high-resolution thumbnail image URL displayed on social posts (recommended size: 1200 x 630 pixels).
  • og:url: The canonical URL for the shared page.
  • og:description: A 1-2 sentence summary explaining the content value of the link.
  • twitter:card: Controls Twitter Card formatting (e.g. summary_large_image).

Why Use Lorem Ipsum Dummy Text?

Using real content during early UI/UX design stages often distracts clients and stakeholders from evaluating layout aesthetics, typography, and visual hierarchy. Lorem Ipsum provides a natural distribution of letters and word lengths that mimics real reading without drawing focus away from design elements.

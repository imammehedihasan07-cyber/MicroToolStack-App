The Fascinating Classical Origin of Lorem Ipsum

Contrary to popular belief, Lorem Ipsum is not simply random pseudo-Latin gibberish. It has roots in classical Latin literature dating back over 2,000 years:

1. Historical Roots in Cicero's Philosophy

The core passage is derived from Marcus Tullius Cicero's philosophical treatise "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (The Extremes of Good and Evil), written in 45 BC. The passage explores theories of ethics and hedonism.

2. Scrambled Printing Standard Since the 1500s

An unknown printer in the 16th century took a galley of Cicero's type and scrambled it to make a type specimen book. The standard opening phrase—"Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit..."—comes from the passage "Neque porro quisquam est qui dolorem ipsum quia dolor sit amet..." (There is no one who loves pain itself, who seeks and wants to have it...).

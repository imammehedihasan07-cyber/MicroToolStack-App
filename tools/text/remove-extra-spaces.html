Why Use an Online Remove Extra Spaces Tool?

When copying and pasting text from PDF documents, scanned files, or web pages, hidden whitespace issues frequently appear. Multiple consecutive spaces, uneven tab indents, and redundant line breaks clutter paragraphs and disrupt clean typography.

Manually deleting these extra spaces character by character is tedious. Our free Online Remove Extra Spaces Tool sanitizes and formats your text paragraphs instantly inside your browser.

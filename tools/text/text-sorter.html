Why Use an Online Text Line Sorter?

Managing large lists, email databases, product SKUs, or keyword ideas manually can be tedious and prone to errors. Our free online Text Sorter enables you to order lines alphabetically, numerically, or by length instantly. It also streamlines data cleaning by automatically stripping duplicate entries.

Key Sorting Modes Available

  • Alphabetical (A to Z / Z to A): Standard lexicographical ordering for text strings, names, and lists.
  • Numerical Sort: Smart numeric sorting that accurately places numbers like 2 before 10.
  • Line Length Sorting: Orders items based on total character count per line from shortest to longest.
  • Deduplication Feature: One-click removal of identical lines to keep your dataset clean.

How do I sort a list alphabetically in A-Z order?

Simply paste your list into the text area above, select 'Alphabetical (A ➔ Z)', and click 'Sort List Now'.

Yes, empty lines are automatically excluded during the sorting process to keep your output clean.

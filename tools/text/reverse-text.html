Key Transformation Modes

Explore the three versatile reversal modes available:

  • Reverse Characters: Flips every single letter, number, and symbol backward (e.g., Hello becomes olleH).
  • Reverse Words: Keeps each word spelled correctly but inverts the word order (e.g., Hello world! becomes world! Hello).
  • Reverse Line Order: Inverts multiline lists or paragraphs from bottom to top.

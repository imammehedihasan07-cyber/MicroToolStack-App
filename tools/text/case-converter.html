Why Use an Online Case Converter?

Accidentally typing an entire paragraph with Caps Lock turned on is a frustrating experience. Instead of retyping everything manually, our Case Converter lets you instantly change letter casing to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Sentence case, or Title Case with a single click. It's an indispensable tool for content writers, coders, students, and SEO marketers.

Supported Text Case Formats

  • Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter of each sentence, perfect for standard paragraphs.
  • UPPERCASE: Transforms every character into capital letters.
  • lowercase: Converts all characters into small, lowercase letters.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of every single word, ideal for article titles and subheadings.
  • camelCase & PascalCase: Removes spaces and formats text into standard programming variable styles.

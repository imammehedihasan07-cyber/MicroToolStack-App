Overview of Supported Casing Formats

Understanding text and programming casing standards:

  • UPPERCASE: Converts all letters to capital letters (e.g. HELLO WORLD).
  • lowercase: Converts all letters to small letters (e.g. hello world).
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of every major word, ideal for book titles and blog headings.
  • Sentence case: Capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence.
  • camelCase: Used in programming to combine words without spaces, capitalizing the first letter of each internal word (e.g. myVariableName).
  • snake_case: Replaces spaces with underscores and lowercases all letters (e.g. my_variable_name).
  • kebab-case: Replaces spaces with hyphens, commonly used in URL slugs (e.g. my-blog-post-title).

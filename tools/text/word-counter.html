Why Use an Online Word Counter?

Whether you are writing a blog article, essay, research paper, social media post, or meta description, staying within strict word or character limits is crucial. Our free online Word Counter provides instant, accurate text metrics without relying on heavy software like Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

Key Features of Our Word Count Tool

  • Real-Time Analysis: Updates character, word, and sentence counts instantly as you type or paste content.
  • Dual Character Metrics: Displays total characters both including and excluding whitespace.
  • Reading Time Estimator: Calculates reading duration based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM).
  • 100% Private & Browser-Based: Your text never leaves your device. Everything is computed client-side using Javascript.

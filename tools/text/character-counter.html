Why Character Count Matters for Social Media & SEO

Platforms like X (formerly Twitter), Meta (Facebook/Instagram), and LinkedIn impose strict character length restrictions on posts and ad copy. In SEO, search engines like Google display titles up to 60 characters and descriptions up to 160 characters before truncating them with an ellipsis (...). Utilizing an accurate Character Counter ensures your message remains clear, professional, and within ideal platform guidelines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my meta description being cut off in Google search results?

Google truncates meta descriptions that exceed ~160 characters or ~960 pixels. Keeping your description around 150–155 characters ensures maximum search snippet visibility.

Does this character count tool support non-English characters?

Yes, our tool fully supports UTF-8 encoding, accurately counting letters, numbers, symbols, emojis, and non-Latin characters in Bengali, Spanish, Arabic, and more.

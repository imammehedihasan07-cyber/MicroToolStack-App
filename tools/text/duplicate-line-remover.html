Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I remove duplicate lines from a list?

Paste your text list into the editor box. The tool automatically removes duplicate entries in real time based on your selected options.

Does case sensitivity affect duplicate removal?

When Case Sensitive is enabled, 'Data' and 'data' are treated as unique items. When disabled, they are treated as duplicates.

Is my text data saved on remote servers?

No. All calculations run client-side inside your browser session using JavaScript. Your text remains 100% private.

What is Flesch Reading Ease Score?

The Flesch Reading Ease Index is a widely recognized formula used to evaluate how easy or difficult a piece of writing is to comprehend. Higher scores indicate material that is easier to read, while lower scores indicate complex academic or technical literature.

Understanding Flesch Score Scale

  • 90.0 – 100.0: Very Easy (5th grade reading level, easily understood by an average 11-year-old)
  • 60.0 – 70.0: Standard / Easy (8th–9th grade level, ideal for blog posts, news, and web copy)
  • 30.0 – 50.0: Difficult (College level reading, suitable for technical manuals)
  • 0.0 – 30.0: Very Confusing / Academic (Best understood by university graduates)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good readability score for web articles?

For websites, blogs, and online articles, aim for a score between 60 and 70. This ensures your content is engaging and easily digestible for general audiences.

How can I improve my readability score?

Shorten your sentences, replace multi-syllable jargon with simpler words, and break heavy blocks of text into smaller paragraphs.

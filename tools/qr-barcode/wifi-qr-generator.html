Why Use a WiFi QR Code Generator?

Sharing complex Wi-Fi passwords with guests, customers, or visitors can be tedious and prone to typos. By creating a Wi-Fi QR code, guests can simply scan the code with their smartphone camera to connect to your wireless network instantly without typing a single character.

Our free WiFi QR Code Generator lets you build secure, standard-compliant network configuration codes safely in your browser.

Best Practices for Wi-Fi Badges

  • Print your generated QR code and place it in guest rooms, reception desks, or common areas.
  • Double-check your network SSID capitalization and password accuracy before printing.
  • Ensure high contrast between the QR pattern and background for fast camera detection.

Yes. The password is encoded directly into a standard configuration string and processed locally in your browser.

No, modern iPhone and Android cameras scan and parse Wi-Fi configurations natively.

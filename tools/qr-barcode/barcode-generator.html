Supported Barcode Standards

Our generator accommodates various global industrial standards:

1. CODE 128

A high-density alphanumeric barcode standard widely used in supply chain and shipping logistics.

2. EAN-13 & UPC

The standard retail product identification barcodes scanned at grocery and department store checkouts globally.

3. CODE 39

A discrete barcode format capable of encoding upper-case letters, numbers, and selected punctuation symbols.

Best Practices for Networking Cards

  • Include international country code prefixes on telephone numbers for global validity.
  • Test your generated vCard QR code with both iPhone and Android devices before bulk printing.
  • Ensure high contrast between the QR pattern and background for flawless scanner recognition.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do vCard QR codes expire?

No, static vCard QR codes generated here never expire and remain active permanently.

What fields are included in the vCard?

It includes first name, last name, phone number, email address, organization name, job title, and website URL.

Why Choose the BARF Diet for Your Dog?

The BARF Diet (Biologically Appropriate Raw Food) aims to provide dogs with a nutrition profile similar to their ancestral diet. Many pet owners switch to raw feeding to improve coat health, dental hygiene, and overall energy levels.

BARF Diet Breakdown

  • Meat (80%): Lean muscle meat provides protein and essential amino acids.
  • Bone (10%): Raw, meaty bones provide calcium and phosphorus (Never cook bones!).
  • Organ Meat (10%): Liver and other secreting organs provide essential vitamins and minerals.

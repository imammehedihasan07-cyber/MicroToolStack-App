Understanding Raw Feeding: BARF vs. Prey Model Raw (PMR)

Raw feeding is a species-appropriate canine nutrition approach designed to mimic the natural dietary composition of wild ancestors. Raw feeding enthusiasts and holistic veterinarians generally follow two primary diet frameworks: BARF and PMR.

Our free Online Raw Dog Food Portion Calculator calculates the total daily food weight needed based on your dog's ideal target body weight and daily activity level, then breaks down exact ingredient weights for both BARF and PMR models.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What percentage of body weight should a dog eat on a raw diet?

Most healthy adult dogs thrive on 2.5% of their ideal target body weight per day. Inactive or overweight dogs need about 1.5% to 2.0%, while active or working dogs require 3.0% to 4.0%.

Why are secreting organs necessary in a raw dog diet?

Secreting organs (such as liver, kidney, spleen, and pancreas) are nutrient-dense powerhouses providing critical vitamins A, D, E, B-complex, iron, and trace minerals that muscle meat lacks.

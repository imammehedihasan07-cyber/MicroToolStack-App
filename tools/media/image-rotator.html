Why Images Captured on Smartphones Render Sideways

When taking photographs using a smartphone or digital camera, the device records physical orientation data inside hidden EXIF headers instead of physically rotating the pixel grid. Depending on the web browser, email client, or photo viewer used to display the photo, these EXIF orientation headers can sometimes be ignored, causing portrait photos to open sideways or upside down.

Our free Online Image Rotator permanently re-aligns pixel coordinates on an HTML5 canvas context. By rotating the actual image matrix 90 degrees clockwise, counter-clockwise, or 180 degrees, your photo will render in correct orientation across all web browsers and operating systems.

Common Image Rotation Operations

Easily correct any photo orientation issue:

  • Rotate 90° Clockwise (CW): Fixes portrait photos that inadvertently open rotated 90 degrees to the left.
  • Rotate 90° Counter-Clockwise (CCW): Fixes photos that open rotated 90 degrees to the right.
  • Rotate 180° (Upside-Down Fix): Flips upside-down document scans or inverted photos upright.

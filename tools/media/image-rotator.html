How Image Rotation Works

Photos taken with smartphones or digital cameras often contain orientation tags that display incorrectly on certain devices. Rotating your image re-draws the pixel matrix onto a new canvas, adjusting dimensions and fixing orientation issues permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to any server?

No. The image rotation process runs 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas.

Will rotating my picture reduce its quality?

No, the canvas re-renders the original image without applying heavy compression.

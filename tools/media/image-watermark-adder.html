Why Watermarking Digital Images is Essential

Photographers, digital artists, and online store owners risk having their visual content scraped or stolen without attribution. Applying a semi-transparent text or logo watermark ensures your brand identity remains linked to your media assets wherever they are published.

Effective Watermarking Tips

  • Balanced Opacity: Set transparency between 50% and 70% so the watermark is clearly visible without obscuring main photo details.
  • Strategic Positioning: Placing watermarks in corners or near subject centers prevents quick cropping theft.
  • High Contrast Color: Use white or black text depending on your image background tones.

Best Practices for Effective Watermarking

To balance copyright deterrence against visual aesthetic quality, consider these tips:

  • Subtle Transparency Opacity: Use a 50% to 70% opacity setting to ensure the watermark remains clearly readable without distracting from the main photo subject.
  • Strategic Positioning: Position watermarks in corners or near complex photo areas to prevent effortless automated removal.
  • High-Contrast Colors: Select white or dark grey text colors depending on whether your photo background is light or dark.

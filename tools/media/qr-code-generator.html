How to Generate Custom QR Codes with Logos Online

Quick Response (QR) codes are essential tools for driving offline users to online destinations. Whether you are creating a digital business card, menu link, WiFi access portal, or promotional campaign, branding your QR code with a custom logo increases click confidence and brand authority.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  • Enter your website link, landing page, or plain text in the input box.
  • (Optional) Upload your company logo or icon to overlay in the center.
  • Customize the QR code foreground and background colors to match your brand palette.
  • Click Generate QR Code and download your print-ready PNG file.

Best Practices for Creating Scannable Branded QR Codes

Follow these expert tips to ensure your custom QR code scans successfully on all mobile devices:

  • Maintain High Contrast: Always keep your foreground QR code significantly darker than your background color (e.g., dark blue on a white background). Avoid light-colored codes on white backgrounds.
  • Keep Logos Compact: Ensure your uploaded logo occupies no more than 20-25% of the central area so that scanner error-correction algorithms can easily reconstruct the surrounding code modules.
  • Test Before Printing: Always scan your generated QR code with multiple mobile phone cameras before sending your print or digital campaign live.

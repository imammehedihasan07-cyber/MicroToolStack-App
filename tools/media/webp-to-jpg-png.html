How to Convert WEBP to JPG or PNG Online

WebP is a modern image format created by Google that delivers excellent compression for web pages. However, many desktop programs, graphics tools, and older applications do not fully support opening or editing .webp files. Our free online WEBP Converter transforms your WebP files into universally accepted JPEG or PNG formats in milliseconds.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  • Click on the upload box above and select your .webp file.
  • Select your target format: JPG for photos or PNG if you need transparent backgrounds.
  • Adjust the quality slider (for JPG).
  • Click Convert Image, then click Download Image to save the converted file to your device.

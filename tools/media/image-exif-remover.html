Why Remove EXIF Data From Your Photos?

When you take a photo with a smartphone or digital camera, the image automatically stores invisible information known as EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) metadata. This meta information includes exact GPS coordinates, date, camera serial number, lens settings, and location tags. Sharing raw photos online without stripping EXIF metadata poses serious privacy risks.

Key Features of MicroToolStack EXIF Cleaner

  • Complete Privacy: All processing is done locally inside your browser canvas. Images are never uploaded to any server.
  • Strips Sensitive Meta Tags: Wipes GPS geolocation, timestamp, camera model, and device identifiers.
  • Fast & Lossless Visual Quality: Preserves original photo resolution and picture quality while erasing hidden data headers.
  • Multi-Format Support: Clean metadata from JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP image formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does removing EXIF metadata reduce photo quality?

No, stripping metadata only removes the hidden text header information from the image file. The visual pixels and overall picture quality remain crisp and unchanged.

Are my private pictures stored anywhere?

Absolute not. MicroToolStack uses client-side JavaScript HTML5 canvas technology, meaning processing occurs entirely in your local device's memory.

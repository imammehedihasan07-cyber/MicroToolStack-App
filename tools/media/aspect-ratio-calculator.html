Understanding Aspect Ratios in Media, Photography, and Web Design

An Aspect Ratio describes the proportional relationship between an image, video frame, or display screen's width and height. Regardless of whether an image is measured in inches, centimeters, or digital pixels, the aspect ratio remains constant.

When resizing images for responsive websites, editing YouTube videos, or preparing graphics for social media (such as Instagram, TikTok, or LinkedIn), maintaining exact aspect ratios prevents distorted, stretched, or squished visuals. Our free Online Aspect Ratio Calculator enables photographers, videographers, and front-end developers to compute proportional dimensions instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I calculate missing image dimensions?

Enter your target aspect ratio (e.g., 16 and 9) and type your known width or height value into W2 or H2. The calculator fills in the missing dimension automatically.

What is the mathematical formula for aspect ratio resizing?

Formula: $\text{New Height} = \frac{\text{New Width} \times \text{Ratio Height}}{\text{Ratio Width}}$.

Is my data saved on remote servers?

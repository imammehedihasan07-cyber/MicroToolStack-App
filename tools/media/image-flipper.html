How Image Flipping Works

Flipping an image mirrors the pixel grid across an axis. A horizontal flip mirrors the image from left to right (ideal for fixing mirrored selfies or changing composition direction), while a vertical flip turns the image upside down.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to any server?

No. The flipping process is performed 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas.

Does flipping reduce image quality?

No, the canvas re-renders the original image dimensions without applying lossy compression.

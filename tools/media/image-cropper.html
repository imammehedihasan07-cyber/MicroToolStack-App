Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server during cropping?

No. The entire image cropping process is executed 100% locally inside your web browser.

Which aspect ratios are available?

You can crop freely or select presets such as 1:1 (Square), 16:9 (Landscape), 4:3 (Standard), and 9:16 (Story/Reel).

How YouTube Creator Earnings Are Calculated

YouTube creators earn revenue primarily through Google AdSense advertisements displayed on their videos. The exact payout depends heavily on viewer demographics, niche categories, and average CPM (Cost Per Mille—how much advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions).

Our free YouTube Money Calculator lets you simulate potential earnings instantly by plugging in your estimated daily views and expected CPM rates.

How to Use the YouTube Earnings Estimator

Calculating your potential income takes just a few steps:

  • Step 1: Enter your expected or current daily view count in the first field.
  • Step 2: Input your estimated CPM rate (typically ranging from $2 to $10+ depending on your niche).
  • Step 3: Click "Calculate Earnings" to view estimated daily and monthly income projections.

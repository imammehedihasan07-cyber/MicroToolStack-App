Available YouTube Thumbnail Resolutions

Understanding thumbnail quality formats:

  • Maximum Resolution (maxresdefault): 1280 x 720 pixels (HD 720p). The highest quality available if uploaded by the creator.
  • High Quality (hqdefault): 480 x 360 pixels. Standard high-quality preview widely supported across legacy embeds.
  • Medium Quality (mqdefault): 320 x 180 pixels. Optimized for mobile feeds and quick loading.
  • Standard Definition (sddefault): 640 x 480 pixels.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail?

Paste the YouTube video URL or ID into the input box and click "Get Thumbnails". Choose your preferred resolution and click download.

Why is the Max Resolution thumbnail not available?

If a video creator did not upload a custom 720p HD thumbnail, YouTube does not generate a maxresdefault image, falling back to 480p high quality.

Is my video search query saved on remote servers?

No. All lookups run client-side inside your browser session using JavaScript. Your searches remain 100% private.

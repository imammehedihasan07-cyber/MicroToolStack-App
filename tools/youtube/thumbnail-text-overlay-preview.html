Best Practices for High-CTR YouTube Thumbnails

Key design considerations for optimizing video impressions:

  • Keep Text Short: Limit thumbnail text to 3 to 5 large words. Viewers look at thumbnails for less than a second on mobile screens.
  • High Color Contrast: Pair bright white or yellow text against dark contrasting backgrounds to ensure readability on small phone displays.
  • Avoid the Bottom-Right Corner: Never place important text or branding in the bottom-right corner, as the YouTube player timestamp badge covers it.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the recommended size for YouTube thumbnails?

YouTube recommends uploading thumbnails at 1280 x 720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio) in JPG or PNG format under 2MB.

Why should I avoid the bottom-right corner of my thumbnail?

YouTube overlays the video length badge (e.g., '10:24') over the bottom-right corner across all search and home feed cards.

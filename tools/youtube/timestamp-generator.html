Why Use a YouTube Timestamp Link?

Sharing long YouTube tutorials, podcasts, or music sets can be inconvenient when you only want your audience to watch a specific highlight. Using a YouTube Timestamp Generator saves your viewers time by directing them immediately to the exact second that matters.

How YouTube Timestamp Syntax Works

YouTube supports two primary URL formats for timestamp parameters:

  • Time Format: ?t=1h2m30s (1 hour, 2 minutes, and 30 seconds).
  • Seconds Format: ?t=90 (Jumps directly to the 90th second).

Why Embed YouTube Videos on Your Website?

Embedding YouTube videos directly into your blog posts, landing pages, or online portfolios is one of the most effective ways to increase user engagement and dwell time. Rich video media breaks up dense text blocks and keeps visitors on your site significantly longer.

Our free Online YouTube Embed Code Generator lets you customize player parameters—such as autoplay, looping, player controls, and custom dimensions—without manually editing complex iframe parameters.

Customizable YouTube Iframe Parameters

Configure advanced player options easily:

  • Autoplay: Automatically starts playing the video when the webpage loads (note: modern browsers require mute=1 for autoplay).
  • Loop: Automatically replays the video from the beginning when it finishes.
  • Player Controls: Hides or shows standard YouTube play/pause bars and timeline scrubbers.
  • Modest Branding: Minimizes YouTube branding and hides the top video title bar until hovered.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I get a YouTube video embed code?

Paste your YouTube video URL or ID into the input box. Configure your preferred size and playback options, then copy the generated HTML iframe code.

Why doesn't autoplay work on my embedded video?

Most modern web browsers block unmuted autoplay to prevent disruptive user experiences. To ensure autoplay works reliably, check both "Autoplay Video" and "Start Muted".

Is my video data saved on remote servers?

No. All calculations run client-side inside your browser session using JavaScript. Your inputs remain 100% private.
